Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are mandatory for verification?

For Indian nationals: Original Driving License (valid for LMV/MCWG) + Aadhaar Card. For foreign nationals: Valid Passport, Visa & International Driving Permit (IDP).

Is fuel included in the rental price?

We operate on a transparent same-to-same fuel policy. Take the vehicle at whatever level (e.g. 50% or full) and return it at the same level. No artificial surcharge penalties.

What happens if there is an on-road breakdown?

Call our 24x7 Roadside Assistance at +91 7981793207. We dispatch our authorized regional recovery partner or provide an immediate replacement vehicle if repair exceeds 3 hours.

What is the security deposit refund timeline?

Most commuter bikes and cars have ₹0 security deposit. For superbikes and 4x4s, deposits are returned via UPI/IMPS directly to your bank account within 2 hours of vehicle check-in.
