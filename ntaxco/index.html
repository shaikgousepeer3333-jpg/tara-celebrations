About us

A personal practice, kept to a professional standard

nTAXco — Nizam's Tax Consultancy — is a tax, accounting and business-compliance practice based in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh, working with small businesses, professionals and salaried individuals across India.

What began as one consultant's commitment to being reachable and dependable has grown into a full practice covering GST, income tax, company registration, bookkeeping and licensing — while keeping the same client-first, always-available approach the practice was built on.

  • Direct access to your consultant — not a ticket queue
  • End-to-end filing, from registration to renewal
  • Transparent pricing, recorded in writing
